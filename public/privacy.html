Zero-Egress Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 • Entity: Neal Frazier Tech (@1nc0gn30) • NullAI Platform

1. Zero Telemetry & 100% Client-Side Computation

Both ZothOS Linux v3.2 and this interactive web portal operate under a strict Zero-Telemetry Invariant. All terminal simulations, Argon2id key derivations, and custom ISO live-build recipes execute 100% locally in your browser. License activation states are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`).

2. Payment Processing

Payments are processed directly by Stripe or decentralized Solana Web3 rails (DePay). We never store credit card numbers or private wallet keys.
